# code_3000
Collection of Assignments for CSE 3000: Contemporary Issues in Computer Science and Engineering

_Note_: Odd-numbered assignments are in HuskyCT, while even-numbered assignments are found in this repo and can be submitted to Gradescope via a Github connection.

## Outline of Assignments

### Module 02: Bot Predictor
Your goal is to build a machine learning model using given data and a Python template. You'll make changes to the GradientBoostingClassifer in mod02_build_bot_predictor.py and then test your changes in mod02_test_bot_predictor.ipynb. Can you minimize the misclassification rate on the test set?

### Module 04: Model Bias
You are given a model that was built to predict a mystery variable using zip code, income, and education level. The variable "group" represents a demographic that is prohibited and was NOT used in the model. Your goal is to use the SHAP package to review this model for bias.

### Module 06: Data De-Anonymization
Your goal is to connect anonymized data back to its source data and check the match rate. To complete this activity, write functions in the "deanonymize" script to (1) link the records in the two datasets and (2) calculate the rate at which you are successful doing so. 

### Module 08: Sustainability in Computing
You will be given a toy dataset and have the opportunity to explore the potential effects of choosing sustainable energy sources to perform computations. You'll code the energy calculation in "sustainability calculations" script, and then you can review the results of various decisions in the "check calculations" notebook. 

### Personal Ethics View
The ethics discussed in Ethical Machines gave me a lot to think about in addition to my own beliefs. As mentioned in chapter five, I do believe that there often are values claimed either by people or entire businesses but if you dig any deeper than the words said, you do not really get an answer. I found the breakdown of the problems with standard AI ethics statements to be accurate, and I agreed with the suggestions and solutions to making better AI ethics statements that not only explain the issue but also the measures taken to deal with it. If I were to talk about ethics at industry level, my beliefs and priorities would change with the industry. On the personal level, I believe two priorities are people's privacy when using a service, such as having their information be anonymous or being able to opt out of features that give up user's information such as their location. My other priority is transparency, within reason of course, people deserve to know what information a service is utilizing to provide them the service, where that information goes and how it is used. While things like terms of service agreements give that information, the writing is so long and complex that it does not give the user a fair look at what they are actually agreeing to, such agreements should be readable in both terminology and length. Accountability, I believe is also important, but I think this falls on the companies, if they make choices such as using an AI, and that AI causes some kind of issues or harm, I believe that the company needs to be held responsible, this would help to encourage responsible and ethical usage by businesses. There are of course many other matters to discuss for AI ethics, but these are the ones I wanted to cover as I believe they are especially important.
